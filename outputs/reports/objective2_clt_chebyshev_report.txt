METHODOLOGY AND LIMITATIONS
=================================================================
The observed product ratings are treated as an empirical distribution. Samples are drawn with replacement.
The simulation illustrates independent sampling from this empirical distribution, not the independence or representativeness of actual customer reviews.
The CLT describes the distribution of sample means under appropriate conditions, including finite variance.
Chebyshev's inequality provides an upper bound on the probability of deviation from the mean.
